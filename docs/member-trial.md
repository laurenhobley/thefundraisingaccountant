# Member trial: ROI.bot prompt builder

Thank you for trying FAC's new prompt builder. It takes about 20 minutes. Please use the **made-up organization** you were sent, never your own organization's real figures.

## Before you start

- **The builder:** https://laurenhobley.github.io/thefundraisingaccountant/builder.html
- **How to use it:** https://laurenhobley.github.io/thefundraisingaccountant/help.html
- **Your made-up organization:** one of these three. Open it beside the builder.
  - [Maple Street Youth Center](../tests/kit/maple-street-youth-center.txt)
  - [Harbor Light Shelter](../tests/kit/harbor-light-shelter.txt)
  - [Prairie Health Clinic](../tests/kit/prairie-health-clinic.txt)

  Each one has a few mistakes in it on purpose. Part of the trial is seeing whether the builder catches them.

## What to do

1. **Copy the answers in.** Type or paste the made-up organization's answers into the builder, one section at a time, and press Next.
2. **Notice the questions that pop up** under an answer. Each time, decide what you'd do as a real member: fix the answer, or keep it as it is.
3. **Label any number.** When an answer has a number, the builder asks where it comes from. Make up a reasonable source, for example "2024 annual report".
4. **Leave and come back.** Close the tab partway through, then open the builder again. Your answers should still be there.
5. **Review and export.** On the last screen, read the finished prompt, tick "I reviewed this", and press Copy prompt or Download .txt.
6. **Paste into ROI.bot.** If you can, paste the prompt into ROI.bot and look at what comes back.
7. **Try another device.** If you have time, do a few screens on your phone too.

## Feedback

Please reply to Steve or Lauren with short answers to these:

1. **Your setup:** which made-up organization you used, and which browser and device.
2. **Confusion:** was anything confusing? Which screen or question?
3. **Mistakes caught:** did the builder catch the mistakes in your made-up organization? Did it ask about anything that wasn't a mistake?
4. **The questions that popped up:** were they clear? Did any feel annoying or unfair?
5. **Labels:** did the Known / Reasonable estimate / Unsupported labels make sense?
6. **The finished prompt:** did it look right? If you pasted it into ROI.bot, was the result useful?
7. **Leaving and coming back:** did anything get lost when you left and came back?
8. **One change:** what one change would make it easier?
